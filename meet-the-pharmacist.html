Meet Your Pharmacist: Dr. Shamuel (Sam) Borukhov, PharmD

Dr. Shamuel (Sam) Borukhov, PharmD, is the owner and supervising pharmacist of Mega Health Rx Pharmacy at 64-51 108th Street in Forest Hills, Queens. He brings more than 10 years of experience across retail, inpatient hospital and specialty pharmacy, and he is fluent in English and Russian.

Experience

Dr. Borukhov has practiced pharmacy for more than a decade across several very different settings, which gives him a broad view of how medications are used from the hospital to the home:

  • Retail pharmacy at Rite Aid
  • Inpatient hospital pharmacy at Long Island Jewish Hospital
  • Licensed medical cannabis pharmacy practice at Vireo Health

He has extensive clinical experience evaluating medication regimens, identifying drug interactions and therapy-related concerns, providing individualized patient counseling, and supporting safe and effective medication use. His background across community, hospital and specialty settings gives him a broad understanding of pharmacotherapy, chronic disease management, medication safety and interdisciplinary patient care.

Why I became a pharmacy owner

I became a pharmacy owner because patients deserve to have a pharmacist they know, trust, and feel comfortable speaking with about their health. I wanted to create a more private and personal pharmacy experience where patients can ask questions, receive individualized attention, and feel confident that their concerns are being heard.

I also recognized a need in the community for greater access to essential pharmacy services — particularly vaccinations, medication education, and preventive care — and wanted to build a pharmacy centered around those needs.

— Dr. Sam Borukhov, PharmD
