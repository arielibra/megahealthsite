Why patients switch to our Forest Hills pharmacy

Queens Boulevard has no shortage of chain pharmacies. What it is short on is time. At a store filling thousands of prescriptions a day, it is normal to hear “it’s out of stock, come back Thursday,” to explain your medications through a window with a line behind you, or to find out at the counter that you have no refills left.

We are built the other way around:

  • We order your medication before your refill date, so it is on the shelf when you need it.
  • You see the same pharmacist, who knows your name and your medication list.
  • As our patient, you can text your pharmacist directly — no phone tree, no hold music.
  • We contact your doctor for renewals before you run out, not after.

How to switch to a Forest Hills pharmacy you actually like

Switching takes about a minute on your end. Fill out our short transfer form with your current pharmacy and the medications you take. We call your old pharmacy and your doctor, confirm everything with you, and have your prescriptions ready — usually the same day. There is no cost to switch.
