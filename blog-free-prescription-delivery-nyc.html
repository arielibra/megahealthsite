Free Prescription Delivery in NYC: How It Actually Works

Free prescription delivery anywhere in New York City, with no minimum order and no membership fee. Here is what that means in practice.

What does free delivery cover?

All five boroughs — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. There is no minimum order value, no per-delivery fee, and no subscription. Prescriptions, over-the-counter items and medical supplies can go out in the same delivery.

How fast is it?

Usually the same day in Queens. In Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island it is the same day or the next day, depending on the day and when your prescription is ready. When you place a refill you are told when to expect it, rather than being left guessing.

Do I need to be home?

For most medications, no. Controlled medications are different: federal rules require a signature, so those are arranged for a time when someone can receive them rather than left at a door.

Is delivery instead of picking up, or as well?

As well. Delivery is an option, not a commitment. Plenty of people have routine medications delivered and walk in when they want to talk to a pharmacist, get a vaccine or have their blood pressure checked.

What about automatic refills?

Automatic refills and delivery work together. Refill dates are tracked, the doctor is contacted for renewals before you run out, and the medication arrives on schedule. For anyone managing several long-term prescriptions, that combination is the difference between a system that works and a monthly scramble.

Why do independent pharmacies deliver free when chains often do not?

Scale, honestly. A chain filling thousands of prescriptions a day cannot hand-deliver them. A neighbourhood pharmacy serving its own community can, and it is often the main reason people switch.
