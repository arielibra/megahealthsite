COVID-19 Vaccine and Rapid COVID Testing in Forest Hills (2026–2027)

Medically reviewed by Dr. Sam Borukhov, PharmD
Owner & Supervising Pharmacist · Reviewed September 29, 2026

For the 2026–2027 season, the CDC recommends an updated COVID-19 vaccine for all adults 18 and older. At Mega Health Rx in Forest Hills you can walk in for your COVID-19 vaccine, get it together with your flu shot, or get a rapid COVID test with results while you wait.

Who should get the 2026–2027 COVID-19 vaccine

According to the CDC, an updated 2026–2027 COVID-19 vaccine is recommended for all adults 18 and older. It is also recommended for children 6 months to 17 years with moderate to severe immunocompromise; for other children, talk with your pediatrician. Getting the updated vaccine matters most for people 65 and older and people with chronic health conditions.

When to get it

Fall is a good time, before winter respiratory season. If you had COVID-19 recently, you may choose to wait about three months after your infection. The COVID-19 and flu vaccines can be given at the same visit.

Which vaccines are available

Updated mRNA vaccines (Moderna and Pfizer-BioNTech) and a protein-based vaccine (Novavax) are available for 2026–2027. Call ahead if you want a specific type and we will check stock.

Side effects

A sore arm, tiredness, headache, muscle aches or a mild fever for a day or two are common and normal.

Cost

COVID-19 vaccines are covered by Medicare, Medicaid and most commercial plans. Bring your card and we will check your coverage first.

Rapid COVID and flu testing

Feeling sick? We offer rapid COVID-19 and flu testing on site, with results while you wait. If you test positive, ask your pharmacist whether antiviral treatment might be right for you and how to reach your doctor quickly — some treatments work best when started within a few days of symptoms. See also cold vs. flu vs. COVID vs. RSV.

Getting your COVID-19 vaccine here

Walk in to 64-51 108th Street, Forest Hills with your insurance card and a photo ID; no appointment is needed. You can get your flu shot at the same visit. With your consent, we record the vaccine in the New York State immunization registry so your doctor can see it.
