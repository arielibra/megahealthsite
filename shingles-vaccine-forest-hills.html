Shingles Vaccine in Forest Hills: Shingrix, Who Needs It and What It Costs

Medically reviewed by Dr. Sam Borukhov, PharmD
Owner & Supervising Pharmacist · Reviewed September 29, 2026

The CDC recommends two doses of the Shingrix shingles vaccine for all adults 50 and older, and for adults 19 and older with a weakened immune system. You can get Shingrix without an appointment at Mega Health Rx in Forest Hills.

What shingles is

Shingles is a painful, blistering rash caused by the same virus as chickenpox. After chickenpox, the virus stays in the body and can reactivate years later. About 1 in 3 people in the U.S. will get shingles in their lifetime, and the risk rises with age. Some people develop long-lasting nerve pain after the rash, called postherpetic neuralgia.

Who should get Shingrix

  • All adults 50 and older, according to the CDC
  • Adults 19 and older who have, or will have, a weakened immune system
  • People who had shingles before — the vaccine can help prevent it coming back
  • People who received the older Zostavax vaccine
  • People who are not sure whether they had chickenpox

Two doses: the schedule

Shingrix is given as two doses. The second dose is given 2 to 6 months after the first for most people (1 to 2 months for some people with weakened immune systems). If more than 6 months have passed, you don’t need to start over — just get the second dose. We can remind you when it is due.

How well it works

According to the CDC, two doses are more than 90% effective at preventing shingles and postherpetic neuralgia in adults 50 and older with healthy immune systems, and protection stays strong for years.

Side effects

Shingrix commonly causes a sore arm, and some people feel tired, achy, feverish or have a headache for a day or two. These reactions are a sign the immune system is responding. Many people plan their dose before a quiet day.

Does Medicare cover the shingles vaccine?

Shingrix is covered under Medicare Part D, and since 2023 recommended adult vaccines covered by Part D have no out-of-pocket cost. Most commercial plans also cover it with no copay. Medicaid covers it too. Bring your card and we will check your coverage before you get the shot.

Frequently asked questions

Who should get the shingles vaccine?

The CDC recommends two doses of Shingrix for all adults 50 and older and for adults 19 and older with weakened immune systems.

How far apart are the two Shingrix doses?

Usually 2 to 6 months apart. If more than 6 months have passed, get the second dose as soon as you can; there is no need to restart.

Is the shingles vaccine free with Medicare?

Shingrix is covered by Medicare Part D, and recommended adult vaccines under Part D have no out-of-pocket cost.

Can I get Shingrix if I already had shingles?

Yes. The vaccine can help prevent shingles from coming back. Wait until the rash has healed.
