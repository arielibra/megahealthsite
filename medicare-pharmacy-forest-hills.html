A Pharmacy That Accepts Medicare in Forest Hills and Rego Park

Mega Health Rx at 64-51 108th Street in Forest Hills accepts most Medicare Part D and Medicare Advantage prescription drug plans. Call us with your Medicare plan card and we will confirm your coverage in about a minute.

In-network, preferred and standard pharmacies

Every Part D and Medicare Advantage drug plan has a pharmacy network. Some plans split it into preferred pharmacies, where copays can be lower, and standard in-network pharmacies. Your plan’s pharmacy directory, or a quick call to us, will tell you which applies. During Open Enrollment you can use the Medicare Plan Finder to compare plans with Mega Health Rx as your pharmacy.

The Part D out-of-pocket cap

Since 2025, Medicare Part D has an annual limit on out-of-pocket costs for covered drugs, which continues in 2027 (PAN Foundation). Once you reach it, you pay nothing more for covered drugs for the rest of the year. The Medicare Prescription Payment Plan can also spread your costs across monthly payments instead of paying a lot at once. Ask us if you have high-cost medications.

Vaccines with no out-of-pocket cost

Recommended adult vaccines covered by Part D — such as shingles, RSV and Tdap — have no out-of-pocket cost. Flu, COVID-19 and pneumonia vaccines are covered under Part B.
