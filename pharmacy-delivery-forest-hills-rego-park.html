Free Pharmacy Delivery in Forest Hills and Rego Park: How It Works

Mega Health Rx delivers prescriptions free to every address in Forest Hills (11375) and Rego Park (11374) — and across the rest of New York City. There is no minimum order, no delivery fee and no membership, and most deliveries arrive the same day.

How delivery works

  1. Get your prescription to us. Your doctor sends it electronically or calls us, or you transfer from your current pharmacy.
  2. We fill it and check your insurance. If there is a problem with coverage or a prior authorization, we call you and your doctor before anything goes out.
  3. We tell you when to expect it. You get a call or text with your delivery window.
  4. We hand-deliver it. Someone at the address signs for it — you, a family member, a neighbor, a caregiver or your doorman.

What we can deliver

  • New prescriptions and refills
  • Refrigerated medications, including insulin and many GLP-1 pens, packed to stay cold
  • Over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, first aid and baby items
  • Diabetic supplies, braces and home health items

Controlled medications can be delivered, but they require a signature from the patient or an adult at the address.

Who uses our delivery

  • Seniors who would rather not stand in a pharmacy line or carry bags home.
  • Working parents who can’t get to a pharmacy before it closes.
  • Adult children managing a parent’s medications from another borough. See our guide to managing an aging parent’s medications.
  • Anyone sick at home who shouldn’t be out picking up antibiotics or cough medicine.

Automatic refills, delivered on schedule

For medications you take every month, we track your refill dates, contact your doctor for renewals before you run out, and deliver on schedule. You don’t have to remember to call. Many patients never visit the counter again unless they want to.

Apartment buildings and co-ops

Much of Forest Hills and Rego Park lives in large buildings. We deliver to the lobby, the doorman or your door, depending on your building. Tell us any building instructions once and we keep them on file.

Local delivery vs. mail order

Many insurance plans push members toward mail-order pharmacies. Mail order can work for some people, but when a shipment is late, lost or left in a hot lobby, there is no one local to call. With a neighborhood pharmacy you get delivery and a pharmacist you can reach, and if something is urgent we can usually get it to you the same day. Read more in free prescription delivery in NYC.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. There is no delivery fee, no minimum order and no membership. Delivery is free anywhere in New York City.

How fast is delivery?

Same day in most cases. When your prescription is ready we tell you when to expect it.

Do I have to be home?

Someone needs to receive the delivery, such as you, a family member, a caregiver or a doorman. Controlled medications need a signature from the patient or an adult at the address.

Can you deliver refrigerated medications like insulin?

Yes. Refrigerated medications are packed to stay cold during delivery.

Can I set up automatic delivery every month?

Yes. We track your refill dates, request renewals from your doctor, and deliver on schedule.
