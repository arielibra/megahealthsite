Our Commitment to Your Practice and Our Patients

  • Better medication adherence, medication synchronization, and refill coordination through proactive patient follow-up.
  • Fewer unnecessary phone calls and faxes.
  • Fewer abandoned prescriptions and delayed starts.
  • A pharmacist who knows your patients and treats every prescription with urgency.
  • Clear communication with your office only when a clinical decision, prescription clarification, or authorization truly requires your input.
Who this helps

You prescribe it. We help make sure it happens.

Your office gets fewer pharmacy interruptions

  • We work through insurance and copay issues directly with the patient and their plan
  • Routine refill requests are handled early and efficiently
  • We contact your office when your clinical input is actually needed

Your staff spends less time chasing prescriptions

  • We handle prescription transfers directly with the other pharmacy
  • We coordinate pickup or delivery with the patient
  • We consolidate non-urgent questions instead of repeatedly calling your office

Your patient gets a pharmacy that follows through

  • We confirm coverage and cost before the patient is surprised at checkout
  • Free delivery throughout New York City
  • We help patients stay on top of refills and medication availability
  • A pharmacist is available when they have questions
