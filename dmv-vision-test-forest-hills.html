DMV Vision Test in Forest Hills: Pass Your License Eye Test Close to Home

Mega Health Rx at 64-51 108th Street in Forest Hills offers New York DMV vision tests, so you can complete the eye test for your license renewal or learner permit close to home and have the results submitted to the DMV.

When you need a DMV vision test

  • Renewing a New York driver license
  • Applying for a learner permit
  • Other license transactions where the DMV requires a vision check

Getting your vision test done ahead of time can let you renew online or by mail instead of visiting a DMV office. Check the NY DMV website for your specific transaction.

What you need to pass

To pass the New York DMV vision test, you need a score of at least 20/40 on the Snellen scale in either or both eyes. You can wear your glasses or contact lenses for the test. If you need them to reach 20/40, your license will list a corrective lens restriction.

If your best vision with glasses is below 20/40 but no worse than 20/70, you may still be able to drive. An eye doctor would need to examine you and complete DMV form MV-80L.

Tips to pass the DMV eye test

  • Bring the glasses or contacts you actually drive with, and make sure the prescription is current.
  • Come when your eyes are rested, not at the end of a long day on screens.
  • If you have dry eyes, lubricating eye drops before the test can help.
  • If you have noticed blurry distance vision, see an optometrist first. You will need a new prescription anyway, and the exam can satisfy the DMV requirement.

What to bring

  • Your current driver license or learner permit, or your DMV paperwork
  • Your glasses or contact lenses, if you wear them to drive

How it works

The test takes a few minutes. You read letters through a vision screening device. If you pass, we submit the results to the DMV. If you don’t pass, you will need an eye exam from an optometrist or ophthalmologist, who can complete the DMV form for you.
