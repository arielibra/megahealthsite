Pharmacy Open Sunday in Forest Hills and Rego Park

Yes — Mega Health Rx at 64-51 108th Street in Forest Hills is open on Sundays from 9 am to 6 pm. You can fill prescriptions, pick up refills, get a walk-in vaccine and talk to a pharmacist, and we deliver on Sundays too.

Our full hours

  • Monday–Thursday: 9 am–7 pm
  • Friday: 9 am–4 pm
  • Saturday: closed
  • Sunday: 9 am–6 pm

What you can do on Sunday

  • Pick up new prescriptions and refills
  • Transfer prescriptions from another pharmacy
  • Walk in for flu, COVID-19 and other vaccines
  • Get a free blood pressure check
  • Get a rapid flu or COVID test
  • Set up free delivery

Need something on a Saturday?

We are closed on Saturdays. If you know you will need a medication over the weekend, call or text us during the week and we will have it filled and delivered by Friday. If you are an existing patient, we track your refill dates, so this usually happens automatically.

If you run out of a medication on a Saturday and can’t wait until Sunday, a New York pharmacist may be able to give you an emergency supply of certain medications in limited situations. See what to do if you run out of medication.

How to never need an emergency weekend refill

  1. Sign up for automatic refills. We fill and deliver before you run out.
  2. Ask about a 90-day supply. Many plans cover a 3-month supply of maintenance medications, which means fewer refills to track.
  3. Let us chase renewals. When you are out of refills, we contact your doctor’s office before your last fill runs out.
  4. Sync your medications. Ask us to line up your refill dates so everything is ready on the same day each month.

If it’s urgent

For a medical emergency, call 911. For urgent symptoms outside our hours, an urgent care center or your doctor’s after-hours line is the right place to start. When you have a new prescription, ask the office to send it to Mega Health Rx and we will have it ready when we open.
