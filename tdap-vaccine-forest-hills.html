Tdap Vaccine in Forest Hills: For Adults, Pregnancy and New Grandparents

Medically reviewed by Dr. Sam Borukhov, PharmD
Owner & Supervising Pharmacist · Reviewed September 29, 2026

Tdap protects against tetanus, diphtheria and pertussis (whooping cough). Adults who never had Tdap should get one dose, followed by a tetanus booster (Td or Tdap) every 10 years. It is also recommended during every pregnancy. Adults can walk in for Tdap at Mega Health Rx in Forest Hills.

Who needs Tdap

  • Adults who never had Tdap — one dose, at any time.
  • Everyone, every 10 years — a Td or Tdap booster.
  • Pregnant people — one dose during each pregnancy, ideally early in the third trimester, to pass protection to the baby. Talk with your obstetrician about timing.
  • Grandparents, parents and caregivers of newborns — whooping cough is most dangerous for babies too young to be fully vaccinated. Make sure you are up to date before the baby arrives.
  • After some wounds — a deep or dirty wound may need a tetanus booster if it has been more than 5 years. For a serious injury, see a doctor or urgent care.

Why whooping cough is still a concern

Whooping cough cases have risen in recent years in the U.S. It spreads easily and can cause weeks of severe coughing in adults, and life-threatening illness in infants.

Side effects

A sore, red or swollen arm is common. Some people feel tired or have a headache or mild fever.

Coverage

Tdap is covered by Medicare Part D (with no out-of-pocket cost when recommended), Medicaid and most commercial plans.

Frequently asked questions

How often do adults need a tetanus shot?

Every 10 years, as a Td or Tdap booster, after one initial dose of Tdap as an adult.

Should grandparents get Tdap before a baby is born?

Yes. Adults who will be around a newborn should be up to date on Tdap to help protect the baby from whooping cough.
