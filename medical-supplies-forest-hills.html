Medical Supplies in Forest Hills: Braces, Mobility Aids and Home Health Items

Mega Health Rx at 64-51 108th Street carries medical supplies for recovery and everyday home health — braces, mobility aids, diabetic supplies, wound care and more — and we deliver them free anywhere in NYC.

What we carry

  • Braces and supports — wrist, knee, ankle, back and compression items
  • Mobility aids — canes and walkers
  • Diabetic supplies — meters, strips, lancets and pen needles (more on diabetes care)
  • Wound care — dressings, gauze, tape and antiseptics
  • Monitoring — items like blood pressure monitors and thermometers
  • Incontinence products

Stock changes, so call (516) 304-5038 to check a specific item. If we don’t have it, we can often order it.

Will insurance pay for it?

Some medical equipment is covered with a prescription from your doctor, depending on your plan. Other items may be eligible for your plan’s OTC benefit card or an HSA/FSA. We will tell you which applies before you buy. See using your OTC benefit card.

Getting the right fit

A brace that is the wrong size or a cane at the wrong height can do more harm than good. Our staff can help you choose the right size, and our pharmacist can answer questions privately.

Coming home from the hospital?

We can fill discharge prescriptions and deliver supplies together, so you or your family don’t have to make several stops. Ask about a medication review after discharge to make sure your new list is right.
