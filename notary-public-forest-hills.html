Notary Public in Forest Hills: Walk In, No Appointment

Looking for a notary public in Forest Hills or Rego Park? Mega Health Rx at 64-51 108th Street offers notary public service on the spot, with no appointment needed.

What to bring

  • Your unsigned document. Don’t sign it beforehand — you sign in front of the notary.
  • A current government-issued photo ID, such as a driver’s license, state ID or passport.
  • All signers. Everyone whose signature is being notarized must be present with ID.
  • Witnesses, if your document requires them.

Commonly notarized documents

  • Affidavits and sworn statements
  • Permission letters, such as travel consent for minors
  • Some powers of attorney and health care forms
  • Vehicle, school and employment forms
  • Some residency and lease documents

What a notary can’t do

A notary verifies your identity and witnesses your signature. A notary can’t give legal advice, tell you which form to use, or explain what a document means. For wills, real estate closings and complex legal documents, talk to an attorney first.
