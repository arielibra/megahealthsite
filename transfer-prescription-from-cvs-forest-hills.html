How to Transfer a Prescription from CVS in Forest Hills and Rego Park

To transfer a prescription from CVS, Walgreens or Duane Reade to Mega Health Rx, fill out our one-minute transfer form with your current store and your medications. We call that pharmacy and your doctor for you, confirm everything with you, and have your prescriptions ready — usually the same day, at no cost.

The short version

You do not need to call CVS, Walgreens or Duane Reade, go to the store, or ask your doctor for new prescriptions. Fill out our transfer form with your current pharmacy and the medications you take. A Mega Health Rx pharmacist calls you back to confirm, contacts your current pharmacy and your doctor, and has your prescriptions ready — usually the same day.

Step by step

  1. Tell us where you fill now. For example, “CVS on Queens Boulevard,” “Walgreens on Queens Boulevard in Forest Hills” or “Duane Reade on 63rd Drive in Rego Park.” Your ZIP code and the names of your medications are enough. A photo of your pill bottles helps but is not required.
  2. We call your current pharmacy. Pharmacies are allowed to transfer prescriptions with remaining refills directly to each other. You don’t need to be on the phone.
  3. We contact your doctor if needed. If a prescription has no refills left, we request a renewal from your doctor’s office on your behalf.
  4. We confirm with you. One short call to check your list, your insurance and your delivery or pickup preference.
  5. Your medication is ready. Pick up at 64-51 108th Street or get it delivered free anywhere in NYC.

Transferring from Walgreens in Forest Hills or Rego Park

Moving a prescription from Walgreens works the same way as moving one from CVS. Tell us which Walgreens you use, such as the store on Queens Boulevard, and the medications you take. We call Walgreens directly, so you do not need to call them, visit the store or download anything. Because Duane Reade is owned by Walgreens, the same steps apply to a Duane Reade prescription. For the full walkthrough, see how to transfer a prescription from Walgreens.

Your Walgreens prescription history does not move over automatically, so a list of your current medications, or a photo of your pill bottles, helps us move everything in one call.

Will my insurance work?

Most likely, yes. Your prescription coverage comes from your insurance plan, not from your current pharmacy. We accept most commercial plans — including CVS Caremark, Express Scripts and OptumRx — plus most Medicare Part D and Medicaid plans. Because your copay is set by your plan, it will usually be the same as what you pay now. If you want to be sure, call (516) 304-5038 with your card and we will check it in about a minute.

I have CVS Caremark. Do I have to use a CVS store?

Not necessarily. CVS Caremark is a pharmacy benefit manager, not a pharmacy. Many Caremark plans include independent pharmacies like ours in their network. Some employer plans do require certain medications to be filled at specific pharmacies, or require mail order after a few fills. Call us with your card and we will check exactly what your plan allows. Read more in using Caremark, Express Scripts or OptumRx at a local pharmacy.

Is Duane Reade the same as Walgreens?

Duane Reade is owned by Walgreens, so your prescription history may already be shared between the two. That doesn’t change anything for a transfer: tell us which store you use and we take it from there.

Will there be a gap in my medication?

No. We time the transfer so your next fill is ready before your current supply runs out. If you are already running low, tell us and we will prioritize it.

Can I move just some prescriptions?

Yes. Some people start with one or two medications and move the rest later. There is no requirement to move everything at once, although having everything in one place lets your pharmacist check for interactions across your full list.

What about controlled medications?

Controlled medications follow stricter federal and New York rules, and some cannot be transferred between pharmacies. For those, we can usually ask your doctor to send a new prescription directly to us. Call with the medication name and we will tell you what is possible.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to call CVS, Walgreens or Duane Reade to transfer my prescription?

No. Give us the name and location of your CVS, Walgreens or Duane Reade and we call them directly. You do not need to be on the phone.

Does it cost anything to transfer from CVS, Walgreens or Duane Reade?

No. Transferring is free, and your copay is set by your insurance plan, so it is usually the same.

How long does a prescription transfer take?

Usually the same day. It can take longer if a prescription has no refills left and we need to reach your doctor, and we keep you posted either way.

Can I keep using my insurance?

Most likely. We accept most commercial, Medicare Part D and Medicaid plans. Call (516) 304-5038 to confirm your plan.

Can controlled medications be transferred?

Sometimes. Some controlled medications cannot be transferred, and your doctor can send a new prescription to us instead. Call us with the medication name.

Can I use CVS Caremark at Mega Health Rx?

Many CVS Caremark plans include independent pharmacies in their network. Some plans restrict where certain medications are filled. Call us with your card and we will check your plan.

Is Duane Reade the same as Walgreens?

Duane Reade is owned by Walgreens. Transferring from either works the same way.
