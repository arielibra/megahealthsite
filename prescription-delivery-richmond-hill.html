Free Prescription Delivery in Richmond Hill, Queens

Mega Health Rx delivers prescriptions free to Richmond Hill (11418 and 11419), usually the same day. We are an independent pharmacy based at 64-51 108th Street in Forest Hills, and for Richmond Hill patients we work as a delivery pharmacy: you switch once, and your medication comes to your door.

How delivery to Richmond Hill works

  1. Transfer once. Fill out our one-minute form with your current pharmacy and medications. We call that pharmacy and your doctor.
  2. We confirm. A pharmacist calls you to check your list, insurance and delivery address.
  3. We deliver. Free, usually the same day, with no minimum. Refills arrive on schedule after that.

Controlled medications are handed to the patient or an adult at the address, with a signature.

Why Richmond Hill patients use a delivery pharmacy

  • No trips to the counter and no waiting in line.
  • We order your medication before your refill date, so it is in stock.
  • The same pharmacist every time. As our patient, you can text them directly.
  • We chase your doctor for renewals before you run out.
