What your numbers mean

A reading has two numbers: systolic (the top number, pressure when your heart beats) and diastolic (the bottom number, pressure between beats). The American Heart Association categories for adults are:

NormalLess than 120andLess than 80
Elevated120–129andLess than 80
High (stage 1)130–139or80–89
High (stage 2)140 or higheror90 or higher
Severe — contact a doctor nowHigher than 180and/orHigher than 120

One high reading does not mean you have high blood pressure. Your doctor looks at several readings over time.

How to get an accurate reading

  • Avoid caffeine, smoking and exercise for 30 minutes before.
  • Sit quietly for 5 minutes, back supported, feet flat on the floor.
  • Rest your arm at heart level and don’t talk during the reading.
  • Use the correct cuff size — a cuff that is too small reads high.

When to call a doctor

If your reading is higher than 180/120 and you have chest pain, shortness of breath, back pain, numbness or weakness, vision changes or difficulty speaking, call 911. If your readings are regularly 130/80 or higher, make an appointment with your doctor.

Home monitors

Home monitoring is one of the best ways to track your blood pressure. Ask us about choosing an upper-arm monitor and cuff size, and bring yours in if you want help using it correctly. Some insurance plans and OTC benefit cards cover a home monitor — ask us.

Already on blood pressure medication?

Ask for a medication review. We can check for interactions, side effects, and over-the-counter products (like some decongestants and pain relievers) that can raise blood pressure.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get a free blood pressure check in Forest Hills?

At Mega Health Rx, 64-51 108th Street. It is free, and no appointment is needed.

What is a normal blood pressure?

Less than 120 over less than 80, according to the American Heart Association.

Is one high reading a reason to worry?

One reading is not a diagnosis. If readings are regularly 130/80 or higher, see your doctor. If it is above 180/120 with symptoms like chest pain or weakness, call 911.
