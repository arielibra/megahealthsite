Medication Therapy Management: A Pharmacist Review of Everything You Take

Medically reviewed by Dr. Sam Borukhov, PharmD
Owner & Supervising Pharmacist · Reviewed September 29, 2026

A medication review is a sit-down conversation with a pharmacist about everything you take — prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, vitamins and supplements. At Mega Health Rx in Forest Hills we do it in a private room and share anything important with your doctor.

Who benefits most

  • People taking five or more medications
  • People who see several doctors or specialists
  • Anyone recently discharged from the hospital
  • People with diabetes, heart disease, high blood pressure, COPD or kidney disease
  • Adult children managing a parent’s medications
  • Anyone who suspects a medication is causing side effects

Many Medicare Part D plans offer a formal Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program for members who qualify. We can tell you whether your plan includes it.

What we check

  • Interactions between prescriptions, OTC medicines and supplements
  • Duplicates — two medications doing the same job, often prescribed by different doctors
  • Side effects that could be caused by a medication (dizziness, falls, confusion, fatigue)
  • Timing — which medications to take with food, apart from each other, morning or night
  • Cost — generics, 90-day supplies, lower-cost alternatives to ask your doctor about
  • Simplification — syncing refills, pill organizers and blister packs

What to bring

Everything: every pill bottle, inhaler, cream, eye drop, vitamin and supplement, including things you take only occasionally. This is sometimes called a “brown bag” review. A list is helpful, but the bottles are better.

What happens after

You leave with an up-to-date medication list. If we find something your doctor should know about, we contact the office with a clear recommendation. We never change a prescription without your doctor’s approval.

How to book

Walk in and ask, or call (516) 304-5038 to choose a time. The review takes about 20–30 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a medication review?

A private conversation with a pharmacist about all your medications and supplements, checking interactions, duplicates, side effects, timing and cost.

Does insurance cover a medication review?

Many Medicare Part D plans offer a formal Medication Therapy Management program at no cost to eligible members. Ask us whether yours does.

What should I bring?

Every medication, vitamin and supplement you take, in their original containers if possible.
